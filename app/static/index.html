Tell us what you're building and where. We'll check it against protected sites and show what each part needs.

1. About the work

Will any of the work happen outside land you already operate?

For example across fields, roads or other people's land. Work inside an existing treatment works or pumping station counts as no.

Will the work take new or extra water from a river, lake or borehole?

Normal sewage and rainwater flows don't count.

Will you need to pump groundwater out of trenches or pits while you dig?

This is often needed for deep trenches or where the ground is wet.

Does your company, or this project, require someone to survey the sites in person?

For example an ecologist's site visit. A required site survey is our A4 step: any part that needs specialist assessment moves up to A4, specialist assessment plus a site survey. Answer no if you'll work from maps and records.
